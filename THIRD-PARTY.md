# Mapbox Earcut

Vendored unmodified ES module from Mapbox Earcut **v3.0.2**.
Source: https://github.com/mapbox/earcut/tree/v3.0.2
License: ISC, included in vendor/earcut-LICENSE.

The generator independently rejects invalid polygon contours, checks triangulation area, verifies every mesh edge has two opposite uses, and requires positive volume. Earcut is not used as a polygon validator.
