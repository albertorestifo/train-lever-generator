How to describe notch depth and asymmetric ramps

A gate's depth on either side is the neighbouring crest lift minus the gate lift. The angular gap sets ramp width. Edit them independently to make one side steeper. A passive cam is reciprocal: a hard uphill approach becomes an assisted downhill departure in the reverse direction; it cannot create a one-way latch.

Cosine is the normal starting point. Quintic has smoother acceleration endpoints but can need wider ramps. Linear blend has a constant-slope middle with eased ends; a sharp unsmoothed corner is intentionally unavailable.

Positive angles move toward Emergency (+58°, negative assembly Y). Confirm which end faces away from you. For initial tactile comparison, reduce the separate friction brake so it does not mask the detents; keep spring preload the same between discs.
