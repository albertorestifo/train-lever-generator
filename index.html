Mechanism and spring assumptions

Only changes the reported spring spacing, not hardware. Fixed interface: 8 mm disk, ±58° stops, 10 mm roller diameter. The generator allows −2 to 3 mm of height relative to baseline (negative values sit below it, for snap notches), not disk thickness. Profiles above the previous 1.4 mm design envelope need mechanical clearance and spring-compression checks; this is not a measured spring limit. Print counterbores upward.
