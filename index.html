Mechanism and spring assumptions

Only changes the reported spring spacing, not hardware. Fixed interface: 8 mm plate, ±58° stops, 10 mm roller diameter. The generator allows 0–3 mm of roller lift above baseline, not plate thickness. Profiles above the previous 1.4 mm design envelope need mechanical clearance and spring-compression checks; this is not a measured spring limit. Print counterbores upward.
